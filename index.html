01 — ABOUT

From site work to structured project support.

I graduated in Civil Engineering and built my experience directly on construction sites rather than in a purely academic setting. That work took me through several stages of real projects — helping plan scopes of work, preparing quantity takeoffs and estimates, coordinating with contractors and suppliers, and supporting supervision onsite as work was carried out.

Alongside that, I developed technical drafting and modeling skills in AutoCAD and SketchUp, which I use to support documentation, drawings, and visualization for the same kinds of projects.

I'm now looking to apply that combination — practical construction knowledge plus technical/documentation skills — in office-based, remote, or hybrid roles: project coordination, estimating support, construction documentation, and similar work.
